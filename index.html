NBA Playoff Model · Prediction Benchmark Python · Logistic Regression · 2026

Objective Build an NBA playoff prediction model as a self-directed exercise, aimed at a Swivr application in prediction markets. Grade honestly against FiveThirtyEight's published forecast rather than against the model's own homework.

Approach Trained a two-parameter logistic regression on FiveThirtyEight's historical game log (126,000 games back to 1946), with pre-game Elo as the primary feature. Split the data by season and held out playoff games entirely; a random shuffle would have leaked season-level information into the test set. Scored predictions against two naive baselines (higher seed, home court) and against FiveThirtyEight's published game-level probabilities.

Result 66.7% accuracy and 0.6259 log-loss on 502 held-out playoff games, versus FiveThirtyEight at 66.1% / 0.6256 on the same set. The two models are statistically tied; the three-game gap sits inside one standard error of the proportion (2.1%). The honest reading is that a straight line through FiveThirtyEight's own Elo rating captures nearly everything their published forecast contains, which is the more interesting finding than any claim of outperformance.

Role: Independent engineering project · in service of a Swivr application

Growth Dashboard · Instagram Analytics React · TypeScript · Supabase · Chart.js · In Development

Objective Build a self-service dashboard that answers which content formats drove the follower spikes on the ARGUS AI Instagram account, which grew from 276 to 3,976 followers over ten months. Treat the underlying numbers as first-class, rather than inferring strategy from screenshots alone.

Approach React and TypeScript on the client; Supabase (Postgres with row-level security) on the server; Chart.js for the visualization layer. Monetary and metric values stored as numeric rather than float. Aggregation handled in Postgres views rather than reduced in the browser. Scope narrowed to ninety days of granular post-level data (Instagram's native retention window) plus a reconstructed follower timeline drawn from monthly reports and Business Suite summaries.

Result In active development. Authentication, row-level security, and the end-to-end render path are proven; the schema is being designed around the actual export formats available from Meta Business Suite. Live demo and performance figures to follow once the data import is complete.

Role: Independent engineering project · personal
